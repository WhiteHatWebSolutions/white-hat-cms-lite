# Code of Conduct

## Standard

Project discussions must remain professional, constructive, and focused on the
work. Respectful disagreement is acceptable. Harassment, threats, personal
attacks, discriminatory language, deliberate disruption, and publication of
private information are not acceptable.

## Scope

This standard applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other
spaces used for project collaboration.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Include the minimum
information needed to review the incident. Reports will be evaluated based on
available evidence, project safety, and the severity of the behavior.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, reject
contributions, restrict participation, or ban accounts when conduct threatens
the safety or usefulness of the project.
